Name + mark as one family: three different strategies

Earlier rounds only varied the letters. These change the relationship. Each strategy shares a real property of the mark: its material (depth and edge), its object-ness (a block it sits on), or its gaming DNA (keys). Mark tilted, name level. HGW Display Black on the left, Fredoka Bold (rounder, friendlier) on the right where shown.

1 · Shared depth

The name is built exactly like the mark: white face, thin edge, indigo 45° extrusion. Same material, same light, same angle. The two stop being neighbours and become one set of objects.

2 · Plinth

One object. The name sits on a block that follows the mark's own corner rule (big curves top-left and bottom-right); the tilted mark rests on its corner like a sticker on a console.

3 · Keycaps

The name is typed on keys made of the same stuff as the mark: grip corner, outline, depth, letters cut into the face like the D-pad. Gaming without saying gaming.
