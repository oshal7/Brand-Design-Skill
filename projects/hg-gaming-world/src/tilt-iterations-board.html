Tilted mark, level name — 12 iterations

The mark keeps its tilt; the name never rotates. Every placement is measured from the tilted geometry (the g block's corners, the mark's highest and lowest points, or its real right-hand silhouette), so the type is tied to the mark without leaning. Font: HGW Display, plus one grotesk comparison. ★ = recommended shortlist.

All iterations

01 · Stepped

Each line keeps the same gap to the mark's real silhouette, so the text edge breathes with the tilt.

02 · Corner-locked ★

Cap line level with the g block's top-right corner, last baseline level with its bottom-right corner. Level text, sized by the tilted block.

03 · Ground line

One line sitting on the mark's lowest point.

04 · Ground stack

Three lines, last baseline on the mark's lowest point, flush-left column.

05 · Hang line

Three lines hung from the mark's highest point, flush-left column.

06 · Cascade ★

Lines step down and right like the blocks do. The stagger continues into the name.

07 · Two lines ★

Happy / Gaming World. Wider, calmer, good for headers.

08 · Under, one line

Mark above, name below, left edges aligned.

09 · Under, stacked ★

Mark above, three centred lines. For square and vertical formats.

10 · Big type

Name as tall as the whole mark. Loudest; last-resort option.

11 · Other font

Corner-locked, set in a neutral grotesk (Inter Display Bold).

12 · Floor rule

A level rule the tilted mark tips on, underlining the name.
